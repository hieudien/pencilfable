## Before you post

1. Make sure the site is deployed and `https://pencilfable.pages.dev/word-search` opens. A pin that leads to a broken page can get your account flagged.
2. Create a free **Pinterest business account** (Settings → Convert to business). It is free and gives you analytics.
3. **Claim your website** (Settings → Claimed accounts). Use the HTML-tag method: Pinterest gives you a `<meta name="p:domain_verify" ...>` tag; add it to `index.html` and redeploy. I can add it for you.

## After you post

- Pin the same image to 2–3 relevant boards over the next days, not all at once.
- Make one new pin per tool (spelling, bingo, maze, sudoku…) with the same layout. Fresh pins matter more to Pinterest than repinning the same image.
- Check Pinterest Analytics after 2–4 weeks. Pins usually take weeks to gain traction.
